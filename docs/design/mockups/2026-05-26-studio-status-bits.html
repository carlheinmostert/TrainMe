Studio status bits — artifact-status row, edit-lock, seal placements

Three smaller pieces bundled into one wave, each closing out a §8 backlog item from ARTIFACT_SYSTEM.md: a per-plan artifact-status row in the Studio header (reads the publish state of every registered kind at a glance), the edit-lock chip in its three new states (paid-only, never on free-only plans — ADR 0016 extension), and the placement of the permanent "powered by homefit" seal + referral QR across all four artifact kinds. The seal is coral, non-removable; the brand-skin subscription adds the practitioner's mark next to the seal, never in place of it.

1 Per-plan artifact-status row

Sits in the Studio header beneath the plan title, before the exercise list begins. One bit per registered artifact kind (handout, player, reel today; poster + calendar arrive automatically when the registry lights them up). Each bit is the kind glyph paired with a state. Three states cover the lifecycle: live (minted & current — sage), published (paid & minted — coral), not yet (never-published — muted dashed). The row reads as compactly as a Publish-gate row but consumes one line of header chrome.

Anatomy

Bit shape — kind glyph + state

Kind glyph (left) — same glyphs used on My Workouts cards and the Publish gate. Wordmark stays short (Handout, Player, Reel) — no per-card vocabulary drift.
State glyph (right) — checkmark for live / published; small dash for not yet. State + colour together make the bit readable without parsing the word.

Three states · one lifecycle

Live — minted-and-fresh. Sage tint (same colour family as rest, the calmer end of the palette). Used for the free kinds that have been minted and reflect the current plan state. Edits flow through automatically.
Published — paid-and-minted. Coral tint, the only state where a credit was spent. This is also the state that arms the edit-lock the moment the client first opens any paid artifact (see Section 2).
Not yet — never-published. Dashed muted border, kind glyph at 70% opacity. Reads as "available but unspent" — no negative tone (it's not "missing" or "broken"). Tapping opens the Publish gate scoped to that kind.

Why this shape

Same vocabulary as the Publish gate. The status row is the compact, read-only twin of the multi-select Publish sheet — same kind glyphs, same state colours (sage = Live row, coral = checked-paid row). A practitioner who's seen the gate once reads the row instantly.

Registry-driven. When poster + calendar light up globally, two more bits appear in this row with no per-plan migration. The row is a thin view over plan_artifacts rows joined with the registry.

One line, no scrolling. 3–5 bits fit on one line at 360px. Past 5 the row wraps; the order is always registry order (free first, paid second, premium last) so the eye learns the layout.

2 Edit-lock chip — three states

The lock only ever activates on paid plans. A plan that's only ever shipped free artifacts (handout, poster) never locks — no credit was spent, nothing to abuse. The lock arms when a paid artifact (player, reel) has been published and the client has opened it; from that moment the practitioner has 14 days of free structural edits, after which the plan locks. Why this shape

Lock semantics

Paid-only. Lock arms on first open of any paid artifact (player today, reel later). Free-only plans never lock — there's no credit to abuse so there's no policy to enforce. This is the cleanest extension of ADR 0016 to the per-kind world.
Plan-level lock. The lock is shared across artifacts — one set of exercises, one lock. Editing-while-locked blocks structural changes; metadata stays editable. (ADR 0016 unchanged.)
Unlock = spend, not buy. The bottom-sheet CTA uses existing credits via unlock_plan_for_edit; no PayFast / Stripe link, no price string, no Reader-App red flag. Top-up happens elsewhere (web portal), unrelated.

Why grace not freshness

14 days matches the practitioner cadence. Patients return 1–2 weeks later for a follow-up; the practitioner needs the freedom to refine based on what they observe. The grace window is the refinement window — it's not a freebie, it's the model.

Open-triggered, not publish-triggered. The clock starts when the client looks at the plan — that's when the value is consumed and the anti-abuse rule kicks in. A plan published-then-ignored stays freely editable indefinitely.

3 Brand-skin seal placements

The permanent "powered by homefit" mark + referral QR appears on every artifact, in homefit coral, regardless of brand-skin subscription. The mark is designed as a credibility seal, not a banner — small, tucked into a corner, never obscuring the workout content. Brand-skin practitioners add their own mark next to the seal (illustrated on the player tile) — never replacing it. The QR carries ?ref={practitioner_code} as link attribution; the QR's destination is always the recipient's plan, never a signup page.

Seal anatomy

What's permanent

The mark — homefit matrix glyph in coral. Same shape on every artifact; size and surrounding text vary per surface but the coral colour is fixed. Non-removable — there is no white-label tier (decision #23, ADR 0022 backlog).
The QR — encodes the recipient's plan URL with ?ref={practitioner_code} appended. The QR destination is always the plan (the viewer lands where they expect); the referral code is link attribution only, never a forced signup detour.

Brand-skin adds, doesn't replace

Practitioner mark sits beside the seal. Logo / accent / practice name pulled from the portal public profile. Never on top of, never instead of, the homefit seal. Both marks visible = professionalism (practitioner) + credibility (platform).
Lapsed subscription reverts the skin. Brand-skin is a credit-denominated subscription (~2 credits / month, ADR 0021 pattern). If credits run out the practitioner mark disappears; the homefit seal continues on the same surface, same position.

Why this shape — the tri-party deal

The seal earns the practitioner referral credits. Every scan that ends in a signup pays a 5% lifetime rebate (Milestone F / M). That's the answer to "why am I happy to leave the homefit mark on my work?" — because that mark is making me money, not just yours.

The seal earns the recipient confidence. A patient holding a printed sheet that says "by your physio" + "powered by homefit.studio" trusts both parties more than either alone — the practitioner looks more professional, not less, for using a known platform.

The seal earns homefit a permanent funnel. Every artifact every practitioner ever ships carries it. The funnel is free, perpetual, and aligned — the same scan that recruits a new practitioner pays the existing one a rebate. Decision #22–#23.
