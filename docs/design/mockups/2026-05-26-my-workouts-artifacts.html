Provenance — who it belongs to

MV Shared with you — a claimed artifact. Carries the practitioner's avatar + name ("From Margaret · Cape Biokinetics") and a View-only tag. The presence of someone else's identity is the signal it isn't yours.
Yours — authored, editable. No attribution avatar; leads with "You", shows a Published / Draft state, and a trailing edit affordance instead of a chevron.

Why this shape

One badged list, not split sections. Mixing in recency order keeps the surface a single timeline; the badges (not headers) carry the distinction, so a freshly-claimed plan and a just-edited session sit side by side naturally.

Thumbnail-led. The hero frame dominates; the kind glyph rides its corner so format reads at a glance without a text label.

Read-only is unmissable. View-only tag + practitioner identity + a chevron (not an edit pencil) all say "consume, don't edit" — closing the "can I edit this PDF?" trap.

Single coral. No per-kind colour. Glyphs are monochrome; coral is reserved for state/identity cues (the claimed-from avatar tint, the Capture FAB).
