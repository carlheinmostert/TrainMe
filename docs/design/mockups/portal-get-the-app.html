Tradeoffs

Variant Pre-publish signal Post-publish noise Real-estate cost Implementation
A · loud Unmissable — full banner + QR + App Store badge Zero — banner vanishes; chips stay as quiet reminder ~90px banner before first publish, 0px after Conditional on plan_issuances.length === 0 (already loaded for audit card)
B · slim Quieter — single-line strip, easy to ignore on a busy dashboard ~44px strip forever, even for users who never need it ~44px constant Static; no data-dependency

Where else the chip applies

The iOS chip pattern carries to other portal pages where app-required actions surface:

  • /clients — empty state: "No clients yet. Capture your first session in the iOS app." + App Store badge.
  • /clients/[id] — header "Capture a new session" CTA (if surfaced) carries the iOS chip.
  • /audit — no chip; this page is portal-native and shows post-publish history.
  • /credits — no chip; credits are bought ON the portal.
  • /network, /members, /premises, /public-profile, /account — no chip; portal-native management.
RECOMMENDATION

Ship Variant A. The signal cost is highest exactly when it matters — for a freshly signed-up practitioner who hasn't yet realised the portal is account-only — and the cost drops to zero once they publish. The auto-dismiss avoids the failure mode of Variant B, which would either fatigue power-users or get permanently dismissed in localStorage and miss the practitioners who actually need it. Keep the per-tile iOS chips after dismiss as a quiet ambient reminder, and let the App Store / QR affordance live permanently on the /account page for users who want to grab the app on a new device.
