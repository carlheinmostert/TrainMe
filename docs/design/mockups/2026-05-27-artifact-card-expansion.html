Behaviour summary

  • Rest state — When a session has at least 1 artifact, a 4px × 5px offset peek card sits behind. Count-independent.
  • Tap session card — peek lifts up + fades; siblings push down (140ms ease-out); artifact cards stagger in (50ms × n delay, spring landing 320ms cubic-bezier(.2,.9,.25,1.2)).
  • Front artifact card — coral border + accent (or practice brand color when brand-skin subscribed). Other artifact cards stay neutral.
  • Accordion — tapping a second session collapses the first. Only one expanded at a time.
  • Tap artifact — workout plan opens in-app preview deck; handout opens full-screen WebView at session.homefit.studio/h/{planId}; future kinds show "coming soon" toast (NOT a modal — R-01).
  • Reduced motion — peek lift becomes instant; stagger collapses to a single transition.
  • Zero artifacts — no peek, no chevron, card looks exactly as it does today. No accidental "Coming soon" affordance on unpublished sessions.
