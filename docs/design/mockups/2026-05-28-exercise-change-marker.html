Behaviour (both levels)

  • Exercise spine: shows when exercise.lastEditedAt > session.sentAt (needs the new per-exercise column).
  • Session spine: shows when session.hasUnpublishedContentChanges is true (already exists — any exercise edited after last publish). The session spine is effectively "OR of its exercise spines."
  • All spines CLEAR on publish (new sentAt makes every edit older).
  • Never-published session: no spines at either level (the whole thing is new — Publish CTA communicates that, not the spine).
  • Reorder does NOT light the spine (structural, not per-exercise content) — proposed default, pending Carl's yes/no.

Rejected alternatives (2026-05-28): "Edited" pill (cost vertical space) and corner dot (too easy to miss in a list). Spine chosen for scannability + cross-level consistency.
