## Single-subject capture (the main bug-fix)

This is THE test the prior algorithm failed. Take a selfie of YOU, no bystander.

- [ ] **6.** Inside your home polygon (Safe Mode auto-engaged). Confirm your client (you) has consent ON and an embedding generated. Open Camera mode → front-facing. Take a self-portrait with no one else in frame.
- [ ] **7.** Open the resulting photo in the editor. You appear **SHARP** (not blurred). Background scenery appears sharp. No coral silhouette, no Gaussian blur covering you. This is the win condition.

## Bystander capture

- [ ] **8.** Same polygon, Safe Mode active. Set up a shot where you (the subject) + a bystander are in frame. Take the photo.
- [ ] **9.** In the resulting photo: **YOU are sharp**, the **BYSTANDER face + body silhouette is Gaussian-blurred**, scenery (walls, floor) is sharp.

## No-subject mode (back-view self-recording)

- [ ] **10.** Place the phone on a stand, walk into frame with your BACK to the camera (no face visible). A bystander walks past in the background. Take the photo (or have someone tap shutter for you).
  - You (back-view, no face detected) appear **sharp**.
  - Bystander face: **blurred**.
  - Bystander body silhouette: **sharp** (this is the conservative no-subject-mode behavior — letting solo self-record work).
