# Brand-skin is a credit-denominated subscription on top of a permanent homefit seal

The practitioner brand-skin — a subscription that re-paints the chrome of every client-facing artifact (workout handout, workout player, future poster / reel / calendar) in the practitioner's own logo + accent colour + practice name + contact — is **credit-denominated** at a working ~2 credits/month, debited from the same `credit_ledger` that already powers per-publish consumption and the Safe Mode subscription ([ADR 0021](0021-safe-mode-subscription-credit-denominated.md)). New ledger `kind = 'brand_skin_month'`. Brand identity is sourced from the existing portal public profile shipped on `staging` with the self-trainer wave (`practices.public_profile` jsonb — logo, accent colour, practice name, contact; see [CHECKPOINT 2026-05-25](../CHECKPOINT_2026-05-25-self-trainer-wave-shipped.md)). The skin is a **live property** rather than a per-publish snapshot: while subscribed, every artifact a client opens renders with the practitioner's identity; on lapse, every artifact — including ones already shared and sitting in clients' phones — **live-reverts** to the default homefit look on the next page load. The homefit **"powered by" seal is permanent and non-removable**: every artifact carries a coral seal + QR with the practitioner's `?ref={code}` link attribution in its corner, regardless of subscription state, regardless of which skin is active. In CSS terms the skin is a `.skin-{name}` class on the artifact root that overrides the chrome tokens (`--brand-default`, `--brand-tint-bg`, `--brand-tint-border`, `--brand-glyph-bg`) but **never** the seal tokens (`--seal-coral`, `--seal-coral-light`), which sit at `:root` scope un-overridden. That single un-overridden token is the visual expression of the decision: monetize *enhancement*, not *entry*. The mockup pair at [`docs/design/mockups/2026-05-26-workout-handout-page.html`](../design/mockups/2026-05-26-workout-handout-page.html) shows the default-versus-Range-Movement skin side-by-side; the per-artifact seal placements are in Section 3 of [`docs/design/mockups/2026-05-26-studio-status-bits.html`](../design/mockups/2026-05-26-studio-status-bits.html). The free tier ships with full brand identity rendered as homefit — every artifact already works, already looks finished, already attributes the practitioner via the seal's QR. The skin is the upgrade that swaps the chrome.

## Considered Options

We considered a **white-label tier** — at a higher subscription price, remove the homefit seal entirely so the practitioner's brand stands alone. Rejected because it severs the funnel permanently — homefit loses both the credibility signal the seal gives the client/patient ("my practitioner runs on a real, auditable system" — an "as seen on" trust mark) and the referral attribution that earns the practitioner ongoing rebates via the 5% lifetime credit-rebate model ([`supabase/archive/schema_milestone_m_credit_model.sql`](../../supabase/archive/schema_milestone_m_credit_model.sql)). The tri-party deal (client gets credibility, practitioner looks more professional and earns rebates, homefit gets the funnel) collapses to a two-party deal where homefit subsidises the practitioner's branding and gets nothing in return. The seal isn't a tax on the practitioner; it's a feature for everyone.

We considered an **entry-paywall** — charge for brand-skin upfront, or gate publish behind the subscription. Rejected as a violation of the artifact system's core principle ([ADR 0022](0022-plan-artifacts-abstraction-before-reel.md)): monetize enhancement, not entry. Free artifacts (the handout today, future poster/calendar) must always work freely; the brand-skin is an *additive* upgrade on top of an already-complete free product. This is also how the platform monetises the free-only practitioner — someone who never publishes a paid player but wants their own identity on free handouts buys credits purely for the skin. Revenue from the free tier without gating it.

We considered **Apple IAP / StoreKit** for the subscription instead of credit-debit. Rejected for the same reasons as [ADR 0021](0021-safe-mode-subscription-credit-denominated.md): (a) StoreKit integration cost (server-side receipt verification, `user_subscriptions` table, recurring-billing webhook handling, lapse semantics); (b) Apple's 15-30% take meaningfully reduces margin on a high-volume / low-price SKU; (c) most importantly, it siloes the new revenue stream from the existing referral-rebate loop. Credit-denominated keeps the wheel spinning — practitioners burn credits on brand-skin, refer other practitioners, earn rebate credits that they themselves spend on more brand-skin (and / or on the workout-player publishes that the brand-skin makes more professional). The Reader-App pattern ([`feedback_ios_reader_app.md`](../../.claude/projects/-Users-chm-dev-TrainMe/memory/feedback_ios_reader_app.md)) extends naturally — credits are purchased on the web portal; brand-skin access is a debit against an already-purchased credit balance, identical to the existing publish-credit and Safe Mode patterns.

We considered **per-publish-date skin retention** — preserve the skin a publish was minted with, so lapsed practitioners' already-shared artifacts keep their skin forever; only NEW publishes after lapse revert to default. Rejected because the renewal pressure is intentional ("looking professional" is the live, ongoing value, not a one-time pose) and because per-date retention works against the always-live artifact model the system specifically commits to. There is no per-publish render snapshot to preserve — artifacts are rendered on demand from the current public profile and the current subscription state. Retention would require *adding* snapshot machinery the system goes out of its way to avoid; the credit subscription model ([ADR 0007](0007-credit-billing-model.md)) is already calibrated to make ongoing renewal cheap.

We considered **a new brand-capture UI in this wave** — a "Configure your skin" surface inside the trainer app. Rejected because the portal public profile on `staging` already captures everything we need (logo, accent colour, practice name, contact). Brand-skin is *positioning* (where each public-profile field lands in each kind of artifact) not *capture*. Per-artifact positioning lives as open question #9 in the source design doc; the handout mockup and the four artifact thumbs in `studio-status-bits.html` Section 3 cover the immediate positioning decisions, and reel + poster get their own positioning mockups when their render infra ships. This ADR does not pre-commit them.

## Trade-offs accepted

Skin lapse reverting *already-shared* artifacts will surprise some practitioners ("I shared a beautiful Range Movement handout last month, my subscription lapsed, now my client sees plain homefit when they re-open the link"). Renewal pressure is the intent, but expect a support-ticket pattern around this and address it with clear in-app lapse messaging plus a grace window (target 7 days, TBD in implementation) before the revert kicks in.

Per-artifact skin positioning (where logo / accent / name / contact land in each artifact kind) is open question #9 in the source design doc and is not pre-committed here. The handout and the four artifact thumbs in `studio-status-bits.html` Section 3 cover the artifact kinds with concrete mockups today; reel and poster positioning ship with their respective render-infrastructure waves.

The ~2 credits/month price is a working price, not final. The mockup uses it as the placeholder. Final pricing is open question #11 in the source design doc — confirm after running the numbers against the credit-bundle pricing (`R250/10`, `R1125/50`, `R4000/200`) and a realistic subscriber count, with a cross-check that the Safe Mode subscription (4 credits/month) and brand-skin together remain comfortably within a typical practitioner's monthly credit burn.

The credit-denominated model continues to materialise subscription state as a `credit_ledger` query rather than a dedicated `user_subscriptions` table; mirrors the trade-off accepted in [ADR 0021](0021-safe-mode-subscription-credit-denominated.md) and is mitigated by the existing index on `(user_id, kind, created_at)`. A future `is_in_active_brand_skin_sub(user_id)` helper joins the same index pattern.
